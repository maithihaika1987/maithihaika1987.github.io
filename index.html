✨ What is Hearth?

Hearth is a groundbreaking Android application that brings real-time live captions and translation directly to your device – completely offline. Whether you're watching a video, attending a meeting, or having a conversation, Hearth adds intelligent captions and translations instantly.

Powered by advanced AI models like Whisper, Vosk, Nemotron, and ONNX, Hearth works even without an internet connection. Your audio stays on your device, ensuring complete privacy and security. The built-in Optical Character Recognition (OCR) even extracts text from images and adds captions to visual content.

💡 Key Benefits: No data usage, no privacy concerns, no lag – just pure, instant AI-powered captions and translations.

Does Hearth really work offline?

Yes! Hearth is completely offline-first. Once installed, all AI models run locally on your device. No internet connection is ever required, ensuring your conversations remain private.

Which Android versions are supported?

Hearth is optimized for Android 9 (Pie) and above, covering over 98% of active Android devices. The app is lightweight and runs smoothly on devices with 3GB+ RAM.
